Our 4-Step Process

A structured approach that helps you move from questions to documented findings and clear next steps.

  1. 1 Intake & Case Review

    • Initial information gathering and case triage
    • Clarify scope, goals, and key questions
    • Confirm what documentation and timelines are needed

  2. 2 Plan & Information Gathering

    • Develop a clear investigation plan
    • Identify relevant sources and stakeholders
    • Organize evidence and establish a timeline

  3. 3 Investigation & Documentation

    • Conduct interviews and review relevant records
    • Document facts, observations, and supporting evidence
    • Maintain organized notes for transparency and consistency

  4. 4 Findings, Recommendations & Next Steps

    • Summarize findings in clear, structured language
    • Provide practical recommendations and options
    • Support next-step communication and documentation
